## v0.1.0 (2026-11-20)

**Features**

- The viewers' `show` and `show_all` take smlibpy parts, sheets, sketches, faces, edges, wires, vertices, vectors, locations, planes and axes in the OCP CAD Viewer family, also beside OCCT objects and at a debug stop, tessellated by smlib's kernel through ocp-tessellate's plugin interface (`ocp_tessellate.plugin`), with names, colors, alphas, every `show` keyword and `set_defaults`, and the tessellation cache keyed by the body's geometry. The entry point `smlib` of the group `ocp_viewer.kernels.show` lets a script import only smlibpy
- Edges sampled by chord deviation: the segment count is estimated from the curvature and refined where the estimate falls short; edge polylines stay within a tenth of the face deviation
- `to_occt`: smlibpy objects as OCCT shapes of OCP or nanocct, to measure them against OCCT objects: `show(occt_obj, to_occt(smlib_obj))`, since the backend measures the shapes of one kernel against each other only
- `SmlibBackend` implements ocp_viewer_core's `BackendProtocol`, so the viewer's measurement tools answer from smlib's own geometry for everything shown: bodies, faces, edges, wires, vertices, locations, planes and axes (distances between any two of them, the angle of edges and faces at their closest points, a box as OCCT's `measure` reports it). It says what smlib knows about a shape, and ocp_viewer_core makes the panel's rows. `SmlibShow.serialize` writes shapes as binary `.smp`, vertices as their coordinates, after a byte with the format's version, and `SmlibBackend.deserialize` reads them in the backend's process, found through the entry point `smlib` of the group `ocp_viewer.kernels.backend`
